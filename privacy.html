Gembound Kingdom — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Short version: Gembound Kingdom has no accounts, no ads, no analytics service and no tracking. Your progress is stored only on your own device. We don't collect, receive or sell any personal data.

1. What the game stores

To save your progress, the game keeps a save file in your device's browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB), or in the app's private storage on Android/iPhone. It contains your chosen player name, avatar, levels, stars, coins, crystals, boosters, collection, achievements, missions, event progress and settings. It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

The optional "On-device play statistics" setting keeps simple counters (for example, how many levels you started) inside that same save file. They are never sent anywhere. You can switch them off in Settings › Privacy & Security.

2. Accounts and sign-in

There are none. The game has no login, so we don't ask for your email, phone number, password, contacts or location.

3. Internet connections

  • Website hosting: the web version is served by GitHub Pages, and Android app downloads come from GitHub Releases. GitHub may log standard technical information (like IP address and browser type) as any web host does — see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts: the game loads the Fredoka and Cinzel fonts from Google Fonts on first launch, which means your device contacts Google's servers. After that they are cached for offline play.
  • Links you choose to open (Help › Report a problem) go to GitHub Issues.

Gameplay itself needs no connection; events and missions run on your device's clock.

4. Permissions

The game doesn't use the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location. On Android it may use vibration for haptic feedback (you can switch this off). If you allow device notifications in Settings, the browser only shows them while the game is open; no push service is used.

5. Exporting and deleting your data

Settings › Account › Export backup file saves all of your data as a JSON file. Delete all game data permanently erases it from your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data also removes it.

6. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect any from children. There is no chat, no contact with other players and no purchases.

7. Purchases

There are no real-money purchases. Coins and crystals are earned in-game only and have no cash value.

8. Changes and contact

If the game changes (for example, if online features are added), this page will be updated before the change goes live. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/jaycee055/gembound-kingdom/issues.

Terms of Service

Gembound Kingdom is provided free of charge, "as is", without warranties of any kind. You may play it for personal, non-commercial enjoyment. In-game items (coins, crystals, boosters, collectibles) are part of the game, have no monetary value and can't be exchanged for money. Progress is stored on your device, so keep a backup if it matters to you; we can't restore lost saves. Please don't attempt to tamper with, resell or misrepresent the game.
